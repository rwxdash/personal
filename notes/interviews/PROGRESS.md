# Drill Log

Yours to maintain. One row per drill session — a second pass at the same
topic gets its own row, so the improvement is visible.

**Score** is questions you'd have answered acceptably in a real interview,
out of questions attempted. Be harsh: "I knew that" is not the same as "I
said it cleanly in two minutes."

**Fumbled** is the column that matters. Write the *specific* thing you
couldn't say, not the topic. "Couldn't explain why R+W>N isn't
linearizable" is useful; "distributed systems" is not.
